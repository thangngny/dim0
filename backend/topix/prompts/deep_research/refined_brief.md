# Creative-ref research guide (wired into clarify + explore)

This is a **guide for agents**, not a rigid user-facing form template.

## Inputs users may provide
- Brief / topic text
- Reference **URLs** (campaigns, moodboards, cases)
- Pasted notes from files (txt/md)

## Clarify stage
- If brief already has goals/channels/KPI → ask **industry traits**, category codes, creative priorities — not generic re-asks.
- Always surface industry characteristics when missing.

## Default research axes (3 workstreams)
1. **Ref Storyline** — cách kể, tứ truyện, cốt truyện, arc cảm xúc  
2. **Ref Tone & Mood** — màu sắc, tạo hình, nhịp điệu, vibe  
3. **Ref Thủ pháp** — thủ pháp thể hiện, đồ họa, 3D, motion  

## Anti-dump layout
- 1 Question + **exactly 3** Workstreams (unless axes override)
- Each WS: few Sources (real URLs) + 1–2 Findings
- 1 Decision + 1 Summary (+ optional Contradiction/Unknown)
- Short titles, short bodies; hierarchical edges; call layout after write
